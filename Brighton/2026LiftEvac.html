Lift Evac and Operations Training

Sunday, October 4, 2026

Check-in opens at 7:00 AM on the top floor of the Rose.

Hello,

Lift evacuation and operations training is Sunday October 4.

This training is not required if you are taking the year off.

Before you come

  • Watch the lift evacuation training at liftevacuation.com.
  • Review knots — KnotsBrighton2021.pdf. You will need to be logged into brightonnsp.org.
  • Dress for the weather. It may snow, rain or be cold. It can be cold in the chairs even when it is sunny. Sunscreen if it is sunny.
  • Bring helmet and gloves. You will not be allowed to participate if you do not have this gear.
  • Bring your lunch and water.

Who you will be riding with

We would like to keep friends together, but this year we have 16 candidates and transfers joining the patrol. Every chair will have 1 mentor, 1 candidate or transfer, and additional patrollers to fill the chair.

Planned schedule

  • 07:00–08:00 Check-In — Top Floor of the Rose
  • 08:00–09:00 Welcome, expectations and plan for the day
  • 09:00–12:30 First Rotation
  • 12:30–13:00 Lunch
  • 13:00–16:30 Second Rotation

Details on AM and PM rotations will be sent on Thursday October 1, 2026.

Thank you,
