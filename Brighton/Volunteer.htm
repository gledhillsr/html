Make a Difference — Become a Volunteer Ski Patroller

Love the mountain? Love helping people? The Brighton Volunteer Ski Patrol combines the outdoors, skiing, first response, and lifelong friendships.

⛷

Thank You for Your Interest!

Thank you for your interest in the National Ski Patrol at Brighton Resort! The Brighton Patrol is a challenging and rewarding endeavor that can truly make a difference. It combines a love for the outdoors, skiing, helping others, pride in a job well done, and making long-lasting friendships.

Candidates must demonstrate the patroller mind-set: a strong work ethic, compassion, camaraderie, commitment to individual patients and to the patrol, assumption of the roles and responsibilities of a patroller, and teamwork. We require an attitude that combines a positive outlook, a willingness to work, and a desire to help others.

If you fit this description and want to take the steps to become a Volunteer Patroller, complete the application at the bottom of this page. Questions? Contact us at brightonnsp@gmail.com.

Your Trail Map to Becoming a Patroller

  • Open House & Ski Test

    Spend a day skiing with the patrol. The training staff will let you know if your skiing or boarding is at a level to continue, or if you need more practice. You must be at least 17 years old.

  • Orientation Days

    If your skiing or boarding meets the expectation, you'll come back for two Orientation Days following the Ski Test weekend — shadowing the patrol from the 8:00 a.m. morning meeting until closing sweeps are "down and clear" (usually around 4:45 p.m.).

  • Outdoor Emergency Care (OEC)

    Complete the OEC first aid course, including professional CPR, a written test, and a practical exam. The hybrid course runs in two sections: Online (June–August, concluding with the written exam) and Hands-On (September–October, twice a week, 3+ hours per class, finishing with the practical skills exam). Three on-the-hill working classes follow at the start of the ski season.

  • Ski & Toboggan Training

    Following OEC, you enter S&T training at Brighton — about 6 weekends on the snow, concluding with a practical evaluation of your skiing and toboggan-handling skills.

  • Mentoring & Sign-Off

    Sign up for patrol days of your choice, work through your mentoring milestones, and get signed off.

  • 🏔

    Full Patroller!

    You made it — you're part of the team. Starting the following season, you're eligible for full benefits (see Perks and Requirements below).

Perks & Requirements for Full-Time Volunteer Patrollers

Your Commitment (per season)

  • 16–18 weekend days
  • 3–5 nights

Your Patrol Benefits

  • Fulfillment of the desire to help others
  • Camaraderie with fellow skiers
  • First-response knowledge, ability, and experience
  • 1st year (training year): skiing privileges up to 3× a week — must be current on training!
  • 2nd year and beyond: Season Pass
  • Ski Pass (credits or family program)
    • Credits (new details coming soon)
    • Family Program (new details coming soon)
  • Canyon UTA bus pass
  • Discounts on ski lessons and rentals (for you and dependents)
  • Discounts on food
